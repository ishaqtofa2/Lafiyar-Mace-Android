LAFIYAR MACE — ADS CONTROL UPDATE

The Android app now keeps Banner and Native content ads inside article pages.

Admin can control:
1. Master Ads
2. AdMob
3. Meta
4. Banner
5. Native
6. Interstitial
7. Rewarded
8. Rewarded Interstitial
9. App Open

Article placement:
- Auto OR Every N paragraphs
- N can be changed from admin
- Maximum ads per article can be changed from admin

Default:
- Banner: max 1/article
- Native: max 1/article
- Auto Banner: around paragraph 3
- Auto Native: around paragraph 4
- Never places two content ads at the same paragraph.

Home, Categories, Saved and Settings do not inject Banner/Native ads.
The sponsor slider is not an AdMob ad and remains available separately.
